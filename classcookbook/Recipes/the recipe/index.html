Dream What You Want

In this guide, we will explore the steps to achieve what you want
to be and what you want to do by this website

You will need: a notebook and a pen

#1 Fix Your Sleep First

Prepare for your lucid dreaming journey by repeating these or two weeks:

  • 1. Go to bed and wake up around the same time.
    Be sure it's early enough (at least before 11:00 PM) in order to have organized sleep schedule.
  • 2. Get seven to nine hours of sleep.
  • 3. Keep your room comfortable and dark.

    • #2 Start a Dream Journal

    Put a notebook and a pen next to your bed. Here's the example;

    "I saw my dad in my dream, and he was wearing a blue shirt."

    Write down anything you remember,

    and try journaling:

  • 1. Where you were
  • 2. Who was there
  • 3. The atmosphere--how was the air humidity?
    What were the expressions of the people you've met?
  • 4. How you felt while you move during the dream
  • 5. Anything strange or illogical?

    • #3 Find Your Dream Signs

    After several dreams, look for things that repeatedly appear.
    It's important to notice patterns in your dreams so that you know you're dreaming.

    Lucid dreaming does not come from remembering dreams, but from recognizing patterns in your dreams.

    #4 Do Reality Check

    You can be asking yourself whehter you're dreaming or not.

    Rather using logical reasoning, actually examine your surroundings.
    Make sure that you look for the signs full of texts or a clock.
    If you can't read it, then you're dreaming.

    Look At Your Body Parts

    Dream hands can sometimes look unusual or distorted.

    Push your finger against your palm and observe what happens.

    The important part is developing the habit of questioning reality.

    Little Adivce: When You Finally Become Lucid...

    Too much excitement can sometimes wake you up.

    Instead, stay calm and consious at the same time.

    Then mingle around within your dream. Enjoy the only moment you can control ;)
