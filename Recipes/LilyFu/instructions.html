Instructions

  1. Soft or medium-firm tofu: 8-12 oz
  2. Ground pork or beef: 2-3 oz (finely minced)
  3. Sichuan chili bean paste (Doubanjiang): 1.5-2 tablespoons
  4. 2 cloves minced garlic, 1 teaspoon grated ginger, and chopped green onion (scallions)
  5. Fermented black beans (Douchi): 1 teaspoon (rinsed and lightly mashed, optional)
  6. Sichuan peppercorns: 1/2 tablespoon (toasted and ground)
  7. Liquids & Seasonings: 3/4 cup chicken stock or water, 1 teaspoon Shaoxing wine, 1 teaspoon light soy sauce, and chili oil or chili powder to taste
  8. Starch slurry: 1 teaspoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons cold water
