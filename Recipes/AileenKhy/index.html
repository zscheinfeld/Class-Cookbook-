Jamaican Jerk Spice I

a pungent, hot mixture of spices used to flavor meats before grilling or roasting.

Ingredients

2 Tbs cayenne pepper

1 tsp cinammon, ground

1 tsp nutmeg, ground

1 tsp allspice, ground

1 tsp dried onion powder

2 tsp dried thyme

1 Tbs sugar

1 Tbs dried minced onion

1 Tbs salt


Procedure

1. Combine all ingredients together

2. Store in an air-tight container
